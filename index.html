Fintech Onboarding Teardown

Where do new users quit when signing up for a money app, and which single step is worth fixing first?

In progress: findings being added Independent study, not affiliated with any company By Jyestha Singh

The question

Where do new users quit when signing up for a UPI, credit-card or savings app, and what is the single highest-impact step to fix?

How I'm studying it

Four steps. Every number on this page comes from my own walkthroughs and interviews.

STEP 1

Walk three sign-ups

Go through three apps' sign-up flows as a brand-new user and log every screen, field and wait.

Not started
STEP 2

Interview recent sign-ups

Talk to 5 to 8 people who joined a fintech app recently. Where did they almost quit, and why?

Not started
STEP 3

Rank the friction

Score each problem by how many people hit it, how bad it is, and how hard it is to fix.

Not started
STEP 4

Redesign and measure

Redesign the worst step, write a one-page PRD, and define the metric that proves it worked.

Not started
