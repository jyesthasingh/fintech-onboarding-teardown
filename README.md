# Fintech Onboarding Teardown

An independent study of where new users drop off in fintech app sign-up flows, and one redesigned step with the metric to prove it works.

**Live page:** https://jyesthasingh.github.io/fintech-onboarding-teardown/
**Status:** In progress. Findings are added as the interviews finish.
**Not affiliated with any company studied.** Interviews are anonymized.

## The question
Where do new users quit when signing up for a UPI, credit-card or savings app, and what is the single highest-impact step to fix?

## Method
1. Walk through three sign-up flows as a new user and log every screen
2. Interview 5 to 8 people who recently signed up for a fintech app
3. Rank friction points by how many people hit them
4. Redesign the worst step, write a one-page PRD, and define the success metric

## Files
- [`docs/PRD.md`](docs/PRD.md): product requirements doc
- [`research/interview-guide.md`](research/interview-guide.md): interview questions
- [`research/flow-walkthrough-log.md`](research/flow-walkthrough-log.md): screen-by-screen notes
- [`research/problem-ranking.md`](research/problem-ranking.md): friction points scored by reach, severity and effort

## Limits
A small sample shows patterns, not statistics, and only public sign-up flows are visible, not internal funnel data.
