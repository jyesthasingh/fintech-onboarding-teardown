# Interview guide (20 minutes)

**Who:** people who signed up for a fintech app (UPI, credit card, savings) in the last 6 months. Aim for 5-8.
**Opening:** "I'm studying how people sign up for money apps. No right answers, and I'll keep your name out of it. Okay if I take notes?"

## Questions
1. Which app did you sign up for most recently? What made you try it?
2. Walk me through the sign-up from the first screen. What do you remember?
3. Was there a moment you almost gave up? What was on the screen?
4. Which step felt slowest or most confusing? (KYC, OTP, PAN/Aadhaar, linking bank, card limit)
5. Did you ever feel unsure about sharing something? What and why?
6. Did you quit any sign-up halfway before? Which app, which step?
7. What did you expect to see once you were in? Did it match?
8. If you could remove one step, which would it be?
9. What made you finally finish (or not)?

## Tips
- Ask "what happened next?" more than "why?"
- Don't suggest answers. Silence is fine.
- Write what they said, not what you think.
